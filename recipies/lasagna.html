Lasagna

Description

Lasagna, also known by the plural form lasagne, is a type of pasta made in wide, flat sheets. It originates in Italian cuisine, where it is served in a number of ways, including in broth (lasagne in brodo), but is best known for its use in a baked dish made by stacking layers of pasta, alternating with fillings such as ragù (ground meats and tomato sauce), béchamel sauce, vegetables, cheeses (which may include ricotta, mozzarella, and Parmesan), and seasonings and spices

Ingredients

Meat Sauce

  • 500g ground beef
  • 50g olive oil
  • 250 yellow onion, finely diced
  • 150g celery, finely diced
  • 15g garlic, minced
  • 150ml dry red wine
  • 425g crushed tomatoes
  • 5g salt
  • 2g black pepper

White Sauce

  • 50g butter
  • 50g plain flour
  • 500g whole milk
  • 5g salt
  • 2g black pepper

Steps

  1. Sauté vegetables: Heat 50g olive oil in a large pot over medium heat. Cook 250g onion, 200g carrot, and 150g celery for 10 minutes until soft. Add 15g garlic and cook for 1 more minute.
  2. Brown meat: Add 500g ground beef, breaking it up with a spoon. Cook until completely browned and no pink remains.
  3. Deglaze: Pour in 150ml dry red wine. Simmer for 3 to 5 minutes until the alcohol smell dissipates and the liquid reduces by half.
  4. Simmer: Stir in 425g canned tomatoes, 5g salt, and 2g black pepper. Reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for at least 45 minutes (1 to 2 hours is better), stirring occasionally.
  5. Make roux: Melt 50g butter in a saucepan over medium heat. Whisk in 50g plain flour and cook for 1 to 2 minutes until it smells slightly nutty.
  6. Add milk: Slowly pour in 500ml whole milk in a thin stream, whisking constantly to prevent lumps.
  7. Thicken: Cook over medium-low heat, whisking continuously, until the sauce thickens enough to coat the back of a spoon. Season with 5g salt and 2g black pepper.
  8. Preheat: Set your oven to 180°C (fan-assisted) or 200°C (conventional).
  9. Layer 1: Spread a thin layer of ragù at the bottom of a large baking dish. Arrange a layer of dry lasagna sheets on top.
  10. Layer 2: Spread a layer of ragù over the pasta, drizzle with béchamel, and sprinkle with a handful of 70g grated Parmesan.
  11. Repeat: Repeat the pasta-ragù-béchamel-Parmesan layers until all ingredients are used, ending with a generous top layer of béchamel and Parmesan.
  12. Bake: Bake for 35 to 40 minutes until the top is golden-brown and bubbling. Let it rest for 15 minutes before slicing so it holds its shape.
